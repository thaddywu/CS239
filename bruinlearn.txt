Course Description

Lecture, four hours; outside study, eight hours. Review of current literature in area of computer science programming languages and systems in which instructor has developed special proficiency as consequence of research interests. May be repeated for credit with topic change. Letter grading.

Class Description

Comprehensive overview of agentic software development frameworks. Exploration of how autonomous artificial intelligence (AI) agents can design, code, and maintain software systems. With rigorous focus on AI safety and reliability, students examine advanced assurance techniques including runtime validation, automated software testing, and property-based testing tailored for AI-driven environments. Class is primarily discussion-based, centering on critical analysis of latest research papers and state-of-the-art developments in this rapidly evolving domain. Through literature-driven approach, students gain both theoretical foundation and practical insights into engineering resilient, predictable, and aligned autonomous software agents.
