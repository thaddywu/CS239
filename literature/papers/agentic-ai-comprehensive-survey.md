# Agentic AI: A Comprehensive Survey of Architectures, Applications, and Future Directions

## Decision

**Rejected for the architecture-overview role.** Verified against the
[Artificial Intelligence Review record](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10462-025-11422-4).

## Rationale

Abou Ali et al. provide a useful PRISMA-based distinction between
symbolic/classical agents and neural/generative agents and argue for intentional
hybridization. That historical distinction can prevent careless retrofitting of
classical vocabulary onto LLM systems.

The course gap is more operational: components, interfaces, state, control
flow, coordination, authority, and failure handling. This survey is organized
around lineages, applications, ethics, and governance, so its architecture
coverage does not answer that gap as directly as the selected candidates.
