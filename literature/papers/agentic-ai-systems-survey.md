# Agentic AI Systems: A Systematic Survey

## Decision

**Candidate as a broad reference, not the preferred overview assignment.**
Verified against the
[Neurocomputing record](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neucom.2026.134049).

## Architecture and evidence

Khapre et al. synthesize more than 200 studies across six dimensions: system
architecture, cognitive foundations, interaction/adaptation, explainability,
security/privacy/safety, and evaluation. Its architecture taxonomy explicitly
contrasts monolithic and modular control; single, multi-agent, flexible, and
decentralized organization; embodied and hybrid agents; and several classes of
tool integration.

The breadth is valuable for lookup but dilutes the control-loop story. It does
not provide as crisp a reusable module-and-interface reference architecture as
Bajoria et al., and it mixes modern LLM agents with broad classical, embodied,
security, and evaluation literatures.

## Course role

Keep as a reference for topology and trust dimensions. It should not consume a
primary slot or replace the architecture mechanisms already selected.
