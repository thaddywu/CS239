# Large Language Model Based Multi-agents: A Survey of Progress and Challenges

## Decision

**Candidate as the compact multi-agent overview.** Verified against the
[IJCAI 2024 Survey Track record](https://www.ijcai.org/proceedings/2024/890).

## Architecture and evidence

Guo et al. ask how a multi-agent system is aligned with its collaborative task
environment. Their schema separates the agent-environment interface, agent
profiling, communication, and capability acquisition; communication is further
organized by paradigm, structure, and message content. This exposes more of the
MAS design space than role-based application papers do.

The ten-page survey is a compact 2023-era snapshot. It introduces organization
and communication vocabulary but cannot deeply treat shared-state consistency,
protocol verification, authority, external effects, or dynamic runtime
scheduling.

## Course role

Best paired with Wang et al. if two short survey excerpts are preferred over a
single new overview. Evolving Orchestration and Information Propagation remain
the technical primaries for organization and causal message flow.
