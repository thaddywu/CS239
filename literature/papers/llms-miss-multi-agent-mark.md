# Large Language Models Miss the Multi-agent Mark

## Decision

**Background position reading.** Primary source: [NeurIPS 2025 proceedings](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2025/hash/cc14cadd13ca5a13ba899cb49351c139-Abstract-Position_Paper_Track.html).

## Contribution

The paper compares current LLM multi-agent systems with classical MAS concepts
and organizes the missing ingredients around social agency, structured
environments, coordination and communication protocols, and emergent behavior.
Its central warning is that several “multi-agent” systems are oversimplified
LLM-centric workflows rather than systems of autonomous interacting actors.

## Course role

Excellent short framing for the ingredient checklist and for policing agent
washing. It is a position paper rather than an evaluated architecture, so it
should orient the empirical readings rather than consume a primary slot.
