# Governance by Construction for Generalist Agents

## Decision

**Rejected as a primary reading.** Primary source: [ACM CAIS 2026 DOI](https://doi.org/10.1145/3786335.3813192).

The CUGA demo places policy interventions before planning, in the system
prompt, at the tool-call boundary, at a human approval gate, and on output. The
typed checkpoints are practical and span a useful lifecycle, but this is a
short demonstration paper with two walkthrough scenarios and limited mechanism
evaluation. AgentSpec supplies the stronger policy-language/reference-monitor
reading.
