# From Language to Action

## Decision

**Candidate as a current landscape review, not as the architecture overview.**
Verified against the
[Artificial Intelligence Review record](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10462-025-11471-9).

## Architecture and evidence

Chowa et al. limit their source set to 2023--2025 work from A*/A conferences
and Q1 journals and cover architecture, single/multi-agent frameworks, tools,
reasoning, planning, memory, prompting/fine-tuning, and 68 evaluation datasets.
The paper reuses the profile-memory-planning-action module map and adds useful
discussion of centralized/decentralized communication and feedback.

Because architecture is only one of seven research questions, the paper spends
substantial space cataloging foundation models, frameworks, applications, and
benchmarks. It is more useful for finding papers than for teaching one coherent
architecture or a technical design tension.

## Course role

Use as an up-to-date bibliography. Wang et al. is cleaner for the module map;
Guo et al. is cleaner for multi-agent communication; Bajoria et al. is cleaner
for one-paper engineering coverage.
