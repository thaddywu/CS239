# The Rise and Potential of Large Language Model Based Agents: A Survey

## Decision

**Candidate as a reference survey.** Verified as the 2025 journal version via
[DOI](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11432-024-4222-0).

## Architecture and evidence

Xi et al. use a brain-perception-action framework. The brain contains language
understanding, knowledge/memory, reasoning/planning, and transfer/generalization;
perception covers text and multimodal input; action covers language, tool use,
and embodied action. The paper then spans single-agent, multi-agent, human-agent,
and agent-society settings.

This is the most encyclopedic of the established surveys, but its scale works
against a seminar assignment. The organizing framework is cognitive and
conceptual rather than an engineering decomposition of runtimes, interfaces,
authority, state consistency, or failure recovery, and the source survey was
assembled early in the modern agent wave.

## Course role

Use as a background reference or citation map, not a primary reading. Its
brain-perception-action figure complements the more operational module map in
Wang et al.
